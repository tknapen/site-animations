# Brief: a bar crosses a divisive normalization pRF (website clip)

For the Knapen Lab website: the page on computational models and the page of Aqil, Knapen and Dumoulin
(2021), PNAS 118, e2108713118.

The author supplied a reference clip (dn-sweep.mp4) and asked for a version that shows both pools on the
pRF, with prfmodel as the simulation engine. Left: the visual field with the pRF as a Gaussian wash, a
solid circle for the activation pool and a dashed circle for the normalization pool, and a bar stepping
across. Right: numerator, denominator and response against the bar's position, drawn as the bar moves.

Silent, about 12 seconds, 2:1, light and dark renders.
