# Brief: size tuning and oversaturation in a divisive normalization pRF (website clip)

For the Knapen Lab website: the page on computational models and the page of Aqil, Knapen and Dumoulin
(2021), PNAS 118, e2108713118.

The author supplied a reference clip (dn-disc.mp4) and asked to show size tuning and oversaturation and
their dependence on the normalization constant d and the size ratio sigma_2 / sigma_1, with both pools
drawn on the pRF and prfmodel as the simulation engine. Left: the visual field with the pRF, a solid
circle for the activation pool, a dashed circle for the normalization pool, and a disc growing around the
pRF centre. Right: numerator, denominator and response against the disc's radius; then d and the size
ratio vary one at a time.

Silent, about 24 seconds, 2:1, light and dark renders.
